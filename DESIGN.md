# DESIGN.md: Ned's portfolio, style "Vintage travel guide"
_Written before any code (webdesign skill). Approved plan: `~/.claude/plans/tender-crafting-catmull.md`. Branch `redesign/2026-11`. Nothing is pushed or merged without the owner's word._

## 1. Mood
A printed city guide to Northfield that turns pages like a slide deck. In 3 seconds the visitor should think "this person is a designer with a point of view", and in 5 know how to call. Warm, confident, a bit playful; never corporate, never a template.

## 2. References: decisions we copy, and don't
| Reference | Copy (decisions only) | Don't copy |
|---|---|---|
| **Nodeck** (nodeck.online) | Full-screen slide stage switched by wheel/keys/swipe, `.is-active` state, bullet progress, bottom status bar, highlighter-draw text, snappy motion (power3.out, 0.15-0.5 s UI, ~0.7 s slides), hover/pointer gating, short-viewport fallback | Their copy, concept, fonts, audio, artwork. No sound at all. No GSAP: vanilla CSS/JS only |
| **PP Neue Montreal** (neuemontreal.com) | Flat grainy colour fields, three full-width hairlines banding the cover, rotating stamp ring, pill nav, giant edge-to-edge wordmark with a small inset image beside it, "staircase" headline with per-word colour, postcards with a stamp | Their coral-red palette, typeface, photos/footage, wording |

## 3. Tokens (locked)
- **Ink** `--ink:#17130F` (text, hairlines, borders). **Paper** `--paper:#F7F0E0` (matches the brand mark's own cream).
- **Fields** (one per slide): `--sky:#6FB4F2` (cover, contact) · `--ink` (problem, proof; cream text) · `--paper` (guide) · `--mint:#8FD9BA` (process). Two ink slides never touch. Pushed to full saturation (the owner's rule: bolder, never calmer); the yellow `--sand` field was dropped because cream/yellow ground with a terracotta accent is the stock "assistant palette". **Swatch check before building:** render the four fields + orange CTA as a test page and look; if the sky or mint still reads soft, deepen them before any layout work.
- **CTA colour** `--go:#C8672C` (the brand mark's orange). **Used for CTAs only** (call pill, send button, sticky call bar) plus the logo itself. CTA = `--go` fill, **ink** bold text (4.8:1; cream text on this orange fails AA), 2 px ink border (so it separates from the sky field).
- **Staircase word colours** (problem slide, on ink, so cream text is the default): `--sky` (8.3:1), `--mint` (11.3:1). **At most three coloured words in the whole headline, plus one highlighter sweep.**
- Contrast already checked: ink on sky 8.3, mint 11.3, paper 16.9; cream on ink 16.3; sky on ink 8.3; mint on ink 11.3. CTA orange vs sky is only 1.7:1, which is why the CTA keeps its 2 px ink border and ink label.
- **Type** (self-hosted, OFL, Latin subset; already in `assets/fonts/`): **Bricolage Grotesque** variable (wght 300-800, opsz) for display and text; **Spline Sans Mono** variable (400-600) only for the status bar, postcard captions and stat labels (sentence case, 14 px or more, no tracked uppercase). `font-display:swap`; preload both. **Five sizes only:** wordmark `min(34vw,44vh)` / 0.8 (capped for 1280x720, 1366x768 and 2560-wide screens) · slide title `clamp(2.4rem,6vw,5.5rem)` / 0.95 · lead and h1 `clamp(1.5rem,3vw,3rem)` · body 17-18 px / 1.55, max 62ch, full ink · caption 14 px. Weight 800 and -0.02em for display.
- **Radius**: pills 999 px; postcards 4 px (paper corners). **Borders/hairlines**: 2 px ink. **Spacing**: 8 px base, page margin `clamp(20px,3.4vw,48px)`.
- **Texture**: static SVG feTurbulence grain at ~6% on fields (inline data URI, no request); postcards are flat: a 2 px ink border and a tone step, **no shadows at rest**.
- No emoji, no stock icons: small inline SVG only (pin, arrow, phone). No slider/carousel. No gradients except the grain.

## 5. Mobile and fallback composition (a separate design, not a shrunk desktop)
**Most owners will open the pitch link on a phone, so this is the primary experience, not a fallback.** Design and check it first at 390 px. Its signature moment is a **postcard pile**: in the guide, each ticket's postcard is `position:sticky` and stacks over the previous one as you scroll (pure CSS; add `animation-timeline:view()` tilt as progressive enhancement only).
- **Stacked mode** applies to: phones/touch, width < 900 px, height ≤ 700 px, `prefers-reduced-motion`, and no-JS. Slides become ordinary full-width "pages" in normal scroll.
- Header: monogram left, "Menu" button right (opens a full-screen list of the four anchors). **One sticky bottom bar** with a single full-width "Call Ned" CTA. No other CTA competes.
- Cover: stamp ring top-right (smaller), h1, intro, then **NED at ~46vw** filling the width, the postcard tilted beside or below it (never covering any text). Guide: tickets stack as postcards, **image first**, then name, style, story, link. Process: vertical route line with four stops. Proof: stats in one column. Contact: form full width, 44 px targets.
- No horizontal scroll at 360-430 px. Fields stay as colour bands between pages.

## 6. Behaviour (slide mode only: `(min-width:900px) and (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`, height > 700 px, motion allowed)
- JS adds a mode class; without it the page is stacked and fully usable. Content is visible by default (reveals are progressive enhancement).
- Keys: ←/→, PageUp/PageDown, Space/Shift+Space, Home/End turn pages; ↑/↓ also turn pages **except** when focus is inside the ticket list, where they move between tickets (roving tabindex, `aria-selected`). Esc closes the mobile menu. Wheel turns **one page per gesture**: accumulate `deltaY` past a threshold (~40), then lock for the 700 ms transition **and ignore the trackpad's momentum tail** (keep ignoring until wheel events pause for ~150 ms), so one flick never skips pages. Test with a simulated momentum burst. a slide taller than the viewport scrolls natively first. Touch swipe is not used (touch gets stacked mode).
- Deep links: `#cover #problem #work #process #proof #contact`; the hash updates; load with a hash opens that slide. Nav anchors and bullets use the same function.
- Inactive slides are `inert` + `aria-hidden`. **Only when the turn came from the keyboard or a nav/bullet click**, focus moves to the new slide's heading (`tabindex="-1"`); wheel turns never steal focus. In every case a polite live region announces "Page 3 of 6: The guide".
- Bullets are real `<button>`s with `aria-label="Go to page 3: The guide"` and `aria-current="step"`.

## 7. Motion (all of it off under `prefers-reduced-motion`; hover effects only under `(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`)
- Easing: `--ease-out:cubic-bezier(.215,.61,.355,1)` (≈ power3.out), `--ease-io:cubic-bezier(.645,.045,.355,1)`, `--ease-pop:cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)` (the one overshoot, for the postcard settle and the stamp).
- Durations: press 120 ms (scale .97) · hover 200 ms · micro 300 ms · text rise 600 ms with 60 ms stagger · **page turn 700 ms** · hairline draw 800 ms · highlighter sweep 700 ms · route draw 1.2 s · stamp 40 s linear.
- **Page turn:** one implementation only, a clip-path wipe from the bottom via CSS class transitions (no View Transitions API: it snapshots the fixed top and status bars and would wipe them with the page). Requests during the 700 ms lock are ignored.
- **Moments, one per slide:** cover = stamp ring + headline lines rise; problem = staircase lines step in and one word gets a **highlighter sweep**; guide = ticket hover sweep + postcard tilt; process = route draws; proof = numbers count up once (respecting the real final values: 8 / 100% / 0); contact = postcard form settles to -1.5° and the stamp "presses" in.
- Animate `transform`/`opacity`/`clip-path` only. No parallax on touch, no autoplay audio or video.

## 8. Copy rules
- Source of truth for wording: `git show main:index.html` (the current page). Reuse only statements that are true today.
- **Remove "real reviews" and every review claim.** The mockups' reviews are marked demo/placeholder, so the portfolio must not call them real. **Drop the "See Reviews" links** for the same reason (they point at demo reviews).
- **Be strict about claims on real, named businesses.** These are sites built on spec. Say so once, plainly ("Built on spec, to show owners what's possible"), and never imply the business commissioned, adopted or benefits from the site. Per-site story = business name, trade, "had no website" (the research premise) and what the sample site shows or lets a visitor do. **Drop reputation claims** from the old cards ("trusted", "strong word of mouth", "busy", "invisible online", "so a driver can find and trust them"). Do not invent results, numbers, quotes or testimonials.
- Use the page's own exact wording for stats: "8 live sites", "100% hand-coded, no builders", "0 generic templates used". The count-up keeps the real final value in the DOM (and in an `aria-label`) so it reads correctly without JS or reduced-motion.
- Style tags (from the rollout DESIGN files): Aladdins "Bold food" · Church Hill "HUD garage" · Lani "Blueprint architectural" · Little Italy "Editorial magazine" · D&D "Ethereal editorial beauty" · Backyard "Match-day poster" · Bright "Bento hardware" · Bang Bang **"Redesign in progress"**.
- No "Line one. *Accent line two.*" formula, no eyebrow pill above a headline, no two equal buttons, no AI-sounding filler.
- Real contact details only: phone `07881 174192`, email `infiniteasura2006@gmail.com`. The form placeholders `07123 456789` and `jane@example.com` may stay as input placeholders only.

## 9. Hard constraints
- **One self-contained `index.html`**: inline `<style>`/`<script>`, no framework, no build step. Local files only: `assets/fonts/*.woff2`, `assets/work/*.webp`, `assets/ned-brand-mark.png` (plus a downscaled ~96 px copy for the pill, made with ffmpeg).
- **Zero third-party requests on load.** Delete the Google Fonts and Pexels `<link>`s (verify Pexels is unused). The only outside call is the form POST.
- Be CSP-ready even though there is no CSP now: no inline event handlers, no `style=""` attributes in markup (JS may set `el.style` / CSS variables).
- **Preserve exactly:** the `leadForm` submit script and payload (name, email, businessName, phone, message → `https://nimble-informal-coin.replit.app/api/leads`; CORS only allows the github.io origin, so the page stays on `desolatemartial553.github.io`), its success/error/status messages, double-submit guard, the JSON-LD `Person` block (real phone and email), canonical/OG/Twitter tags, favicon + apple-touch icon, skip link, `<main>`, `lang`, the accessibility fixes from the Sept ECC audit, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`. Update the `<title>`/description only if it keeps "8 live projects" true.
- Screenshots: `assets/work/<slug>.webp`, 960x600, with `width`/`height` attributes, `decoding="async"`; in stacked mode `loading="lazy"` (the cover's Aladdins image eager). **In slide mode, preload all eight on first idle** (332 KB total) so a page turn never shows an empty postcard. and meaningful alt text describing what is visible.
- **Viewport matrix:** 390, 768, 1280x720, 1366x768, 1440x900, 1920x1080, 2560x1440 and 200% zoom; every headline stays within two lines (the staircase is the one documented exception and may take up to four lines on a phone).
- Anchors clear the fixed header (`scroll-margin-top`) in stacked mode. Console clean. No horizontal scroll at 390 px. Lighthouse mobile a11y/best-practices/SEO 100; performance at or above the old baseline.
- Fonts' source and licence are recorded below.

## 12. The idea and the live thing (design-taste-frontend)
- **Idea sentence:** on this page, **the eight real Northfield sites Ned built** become **postcards in a visitor's guide to the neighbourhood**, stamped with a local postmark. Competitor test: another Birmingham designer cannot say this; they have no Northfield postmark and no eight real local stops.
- **One live thing:** type your business name and watch it become a postcard stamp (Contact slide). Uses the visitor's own words, works with a thumb, has a plain fallback, never blocks reading, and feeds the form's Business Name field.

## 13. Deliberate exceptions to the taste linter (brief-driven, not defaults)
`design-taste-frontend` bans hairline rules, monospace, index numbers, tiny uppercase labels, floating pill nav and styled accent words. The owner chose Nodeck and PP Neue Montreal, and several of those devices are the reference. So these stay, **narrowly**, and must not be "fixed" away or spread: (1) the three cover hairlines and the route line (the only rules on the page); (2) Spline Sans Mono in three places only (status bar, postcard captions, stat labels); (3) the status bar's "3 / 6" counter (a real progress indicator; tickets carry no numerals); (4) up to three coloured words on the problem headline plus one highlighter sweep (Nodeck/Neue Montreal device); (5) the brand orange on cream is Ned's own logo, kept as an existing-brand asset. Everything else follows the linter.
